Child safety standards

Last updated 15 November 2026

Oathlight has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). This page states our standards and how to reach us.

1 · What is prohibited

Any content or behaviour that sexually exploits, abuses or endangers children is prohibited on Oathlight. That includes child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sextortion, the sexualisation of minors, and any attempt to use Oathlight to contact a child for such a purpose.

An account involved in any of this is removed permanently.

2 · How the app is built to prevent it

  • Parties — the only place members meet — are closed to anyone under 18, using Google Play's age signal or a neutral age check.
  • There is no text, no images, no voice, no links and no profiles. Members can exchange only thirteen built-in marks.
  • Members are anonymous to each other and cannot find, follow or contact one another outside a party.
  • A party dissolves after forty-eight hours and cannot be extended.
  • Parties can be switched off for everyone at once if we ever need to.

3 · Reporting

Inside a party, every member can be reported from the party screen; no typing is needed. Reports are reviewed by a person.

You can also write to safety@oathlight.app at any time, with or without an account.

4 · What we do with a report

We review it, remove the member from parties or from Oathlight where warranted, and keep the report for safety review even if an account is later deleted.

Where we become aware of child sexual abuse material or a child in danger, we report it to the competent authorities — the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and the relevant national law-enforcement body — and we comply with applicable child safety laws.

5 · Point of contact

For child safety matters, including enquiries from Google Play and from authorities: Oleh Okhrimenko · safety@oathlight.app.
