Delete your account

This page explains what deletion removes and how to ask for it. Nothing here tries to talk you out of it.

Fastest: delete it in the app

If you still have Oathlight installed, this takes about ten seconds and needs no email from us.

Hero→Account→Delete account

Request it without the app

For people who no longer have the app installed. Use the address the account was created with, so we can match the request to it.

You can also write to delete@oathlight.app from the account address. Either way reaches the same person.

What is removed

  • Your heroes and every form they have taken
  • Levels and all experience earned
  • Your whole task history, past and planned, with its notes and reasons
  • Party membership and the marks you sent
  • Your settings, reminders and notification tokens
  • The record of your subscription (the subscription itself stays with Google — see below)
  • The account itself and its email address

This is permanent. There is no export, and nothing can be rebuilt from our side once the thirty days have passed.

One thing is kept: if you reported a party member, or were reported, that report stays for safety review, detached from your account.

Deleting the account does not cancel a subscription

A subscription bought through Google Play is held by Google, not by us — deleting your Oathlight account leaves it running, and Google keeps charging for it. Cancel it separately in the Play Store: Play Store → your profile → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions.

Timing

7 daysto confirm a request made on this page — usually the same day. In the app it is immediate.
30 daysto change your mind — sign in again in that window and everything returns untouched
After thatthe data is erased from live systems, and from backups within a further 30 days
