What makes it different

A character that changes

Five forms on the way to level 42. Not a bar that fills — a shape that shifts, slowly enough that you notice when it does.

Nothing decays

No damage, no lost levels, no streak to break. A missed day is a day you did not move, and that is all it is.

Parties that end

Forty-eight hours beside two other wanderers, then it dissolves. Thirteen marks, no words. Nothing to type means no cruelty and no small talk.

One large, three medium, five small

The 1-3-5 day. Nine slots, and the day is full when they are full — not when the list is empty.

How it works

I

Set the day

One large task, three medium, five small. It takes a minute and it is the only planning the app asks of you.

II

Do the ordinary things

Every task you finish is weight behind the character. Larger tasks carry more, as they should.

III

Sit by a fire, or don't

An anonymous party of three for forty-eight hours, marks only, adults only. When it ends it is gone, and nobody is waiting on you.

IV

Come back whenever

After a day or after a month, your character is exactly where you left it. Progress waits.

One hero, five forms

The Strategist, from the first level to the fortieth. Every ten levels the hero changes shape.
